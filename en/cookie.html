Cookie policy

In effect from 1 September 2026 · Applies to the emimate.it website

This site uses no cookies. It sets none of its own, allows none from third parties and resorts to no equivalent technology. For that reason no consent banner is shown: there is nothing to accept or refuse.

1. What this document covers

This document concerns the website emimate.it, that is, the pages being read right now. The Emimate app for Android is a separate program with its own handling of data, described in the privacy policy. In short: the app does not even request permission to access the internet, and transmits nothing to any server.

2. No cookies of any kind

No technical cookies, analytics cookies, profiling or advertising cookies are used, whether first-party or third-party. The site consists of static pages that require no sign-in, hold no session, have no forms to fill in and have no preferences to remember between one visit and the next: no function here needs a cookie in order to work.

The law requires the user's prior consent only where cookies or equivalent tracking tools are present. In the absence of both, there is no consent to collect, and a banner asking for it would have no subject matter.

3. No equivalent technology

The absence of cookies is not made up for by other means. The site does not use localStorage or sessionStorage, employs no browser or device fingerprinting, and embeds no tracking pixels, web beacons, maps, videos or social network share buttons.

Every file needed to display these pages — the stylesheet, the typeface, the logo and the images — is hosted on this same site. The Manrope typeface in particular is served from here rather than from Google Fonts precisely so that the visitor's IP address is not disclosed to an outside provider. The result is that no third party learns of the visit.

4. The only script present

The two presentation pages load a single JavaScript file, assets/promo.js, whose purpose is to work out how many days remain until the app is published on Google Play and to write that number into the band at the top.

The script reads only the date set on the device, replaces a sentence in the page and stops. It writes no cookie, uses no form of local storage, opens no network connection and transmits nothing anywhere. It is served from this site, it is a few lines long, and it can be read in full at assets/promo.js.

5. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. The service's documentation states expressly that, when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor holds a GitHub account or has signed in to one.

These are the provider's internal records, not cookies, and they are not accessible to the author of the site, who has no visitor statistics of any kind. It should be noted that GitHub publishes no express statement as to the absence of cookies on the static pages it serves; the absence of cookies on this site can nonetheless be established directly by anyone, through the check described in section 7. The processing carried out by GitHub is governed by the GitHub general privacy statement.

6. Links to other sites

Some pages contain links to external sites, such as Google Play or the site of the Italian data protection authority. These are plain links: nothing is loaded from those sites unless you choose to visit them. Once a link is followed, the policies of the destination site apply, and this document has no bearing on them.

7. How to check

None of the above has to be taken on trust. In most browsers it is enough to open the developer tools with F12 and look at the storage section — Application in Chrome and Edge, Storage in Firefox — to see that the Cookies, Local storage and Session storage entries for this site are empty. The Network tab further shows that every request the page makes goes to this same domain.

8. Changes

Should the site ever introduce a cookie or a tracking tool, this document would be updated and, where the law requires it, prior consent would be requested before anything was installed. Each updated version is published at this address, with a new date at the top.
