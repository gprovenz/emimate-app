The headache diary you fill in with a single tap.

One tap records the onset of an attack, a second tap records its conclusion. Every other detail is optional: the information may be entered whenever it is most convenient, during the attack, at its end or days later.

How the project began

Emimate was developed by someone who lives with headaches every day. During an attack, completing a detailed form is hardly manageable, and a diary that requires too many steps inevitably remains incomplete. Emimate requires a single action and defers every other entry to a more suitable moment.

Simplicity

An immediate action, the details afterwards

The main screen presents a single button: it is pressed at the onset of the attack and pressed again at its conclusion. The attack is thereby recorded; no further field is required.

  • Intensity, pain location, symptoms, medications taken with the corresponding number of doses, possible triggers and a free-form note.
  • The information may be entered during the attack, immediately afterwards or days later: the timing is determined by the user, not by the application.
  • During an attack a notification displays the timer and the command to close it; a home screen widget provides the same function.
  • Where an attack is recorded at a later point, the date and time may be selected from the calendar.

Pain location

The pain location, marked on a map of the head

Fifteen areas, front and back. Left and right refer to the user, not to the screen. The selected areas enter the report and the export along with all other data; in the report they are summarised on two representations of the head, where darker areas indicate where the pain recurred most often.

History

A concise view of the month

Every attack is retained with its times, duration and intensity. The calendar shades the days according to the intensity recorded and summarises the month in three figures: number of attacks, total duration and average duration.

Themes and colours

Configurable themes and colour scales

A light theme, a dark theme or the one set by the system, with several colour scales for intensity. Where light is hard to bear, the dark theme is generally preferable during an attack.

Report

A document intended for the medical consultation

The PDF export is not a mere list of data: it is a laid-out A4 document comprising a header, summary figures, a calendar month by month, the pain location and the full table of attacks with times, duration, symptoms, medications, triggers and notes.

  • The period is selectable: the last 3, 6 or 12 months, a custom range, or the whole diary.
  • The file may be opened in another application or shared like any other document.
  • The same data may also be exported in CSV format, for use in a spreadsheet.

Verifiable privacy

Emimate does not request permission to access the internet, a fact anyone can verify in the app's permission list: the app is in no position to transmit anything. No server of the developer's, no collection of usage statistics, no advertising.

One backup copy does leave the device, and it is Android that takes it: with the feature on, the system keeps the diary in the user's own Google Drive, so that losing the phone does not mean losing the diary. The copy stays in the user's account, and the developer does not reach it. The feature is switched off in the settings. Instead, or as well, the whole diary may be exported to a CSV file and subsequently reimported.

Privacy policy

Free, with no exceptions

No in-app purchases, no subscription, no feature reserved for a paid version and no advertising of any kind.

Five languages

The interface and the report are available in Italian, English, French, Spanish and German, with the language selected inside the app.

Beta testing

The app is in its test phase

Emimate is currently distributed as a trial version to a small group of users, ahead of publication. Anyone wishing to take part, to use the app before its release and to report any problems, may ask to do so by sending an email.

Ask to take part

Emimate is a diary: it records the information entered by the user and returns it. It does not diagnose, it does not provide medical or therapeutic advice and it does not constitute a medical device. Any decision concerning your health should be taken with your doctor.
