Privacy policy

Version 1 · Effective 1 September 2026 · Applies to Emimate for Android, package com.emimate.app, version 1.0.0 and later

Emimate collects no personal data. All information recorded by the user remains in the app's private storage, on the device. The developer neither receives nor holds any data, and has no technical means of obtaining it.

1. Controller and scope

Emimate is published by an independent developer, reachable at gprovenz.dev@gmail.com. Because the app transmits no information and the developer operates no server, no processing of personal data is carried out by the developer, and there is accordingly no processing for which a lawful basis under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 would need to be identified. The diary data is processed solely by the user, on the user's own device and under the user's own control.

This policy concerns the Emimate app for Android. The emimate.it website is the subject of a separate document, the cookie policy, which describes how it operates and records the absence of cookies and tracking tools.

2. Data recorded by the app, and where it is held

InformationWhere it is keptPurpose
The headache diary: the start and end of each attack, together with any intensity, medication, triggers and notes entered by the user A database held in the app's private storage, on the device Displaying the diary, the monthly figures and the report
The settings: interface language, theme, and the name to be printed on the report The same private storage Retaining the configuration chosen by the user

Diary entries describe the user's state of health, which Regulation (EU) 2016/679 treats as a special category of personal data. It is precisely for that reason that the app is designed so that such information never leaves the device, unless the user arranges its transfer. No other app is able to access that storage, nor can the developer. There is no account, no sign-in procedure and no server.

3. The technical basis for the statements above

Emimate does not request the INTERNET permission. Android enforces this: without that permission the operating system does not allow the app to open any network connection. This is not a setting that can be altered silently on an installed app; it is a declaration contained in the manifest, which anyone can extract from the installed package and verify.

Android's automatic backup is also disabled (allowBackup="false"), so the system does not copy the diary to Google Drive.

The app integrates no advertising network, no analytics library, no crash reporting service and no third-party SDK. No party is informed that the app has been opened, or when.

4. Permissions requested

PermissionPurpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To display the notification with the timer and the End command while an attack is in progress. The permission may be refused; the remaining functions stay available.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To restore that notification and the widget after the device restarts, so that an attack begun before the restart is still shown as in progress.

The app requests no further permission. It does not request access to storage, contacts, location, the camera or the microphone. The reading and writing of CSV and PDF files takes place through the system file picker, which grants access to the single file indicated by the user.

5. Transfers arranged by the user

Emimate allows the diary to be exported in CSV format and a report to be produced in PDF format for the user's doctor, and allows either to be passed to another app through the Android share function. This occurs solely at the user's request: the user determines the moment, the period covered, where the file is placed and who receives it.

Once exported, the file is an ordinary document under the user's control and is no longer governed by this policy. Its subsequent handling is subject to the terms of the app, mail service or storage service selected.

6. Retention period

The diary remains on the device until deleted by the user. There is no expiry and no automatic clean-up, as no information is held elsewhere and there is no data for the developer to retain or erase.

7. Erasure of data

  • A single attack: open it in the app and select Delete.
  • The whole diary: uninstall the app. Android removes its private storage at the same time. In the absence of backups and of copies on any server, the erasure is complete and final. The same result is obtained from the Android settings, under Apps → Emimate → Storage → Clear storage, where the app is to be kept installed.

Restoring a backup never entails an erasure: it adds the attacks contained in the file, updates those the file describes differently, and leaves the remainder unaltered. Erasure is always an explicit act.

8. Rights of the data subject

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 affords the data subject the right of access to their personal data, together with the rights to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability. As the developer holds none of the user's data, those rights are exercised directly on the device and require no request to be submitted to anyone: the diary is fully viewable in the app, editable entry by entry, erasable as set out above, and exportable at any time to a CSV file in a documented, open format that any spreadsheet can read.

The data subject also has the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy that authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

9. Transfers to third countries

None take place. As no data is transmitted, no transfer occurs, either within or outside the European Economic Area.

10. Children

Emimate is not directed at children and collects no information from any user, of any age.

11. Medical disclaimer

Emimate is a diary: it records the information entered by the user and returns it. It does not diagnose, it does not provide medical advice and it does not constitute a medical device. Decisions concerning the user's health rest with their doctor.

12. Changes to this policy

Each new version is published at this address, with a new version number and a new date at the top. As the app collects no data, any change would concern clarity of expression or a new feature of the app, rather than the introduction of newly collected data. Should a future version of the app ever collect or transmit information, the change would be described here before taking effect.
